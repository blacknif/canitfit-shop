If the button does nothing

It opens the Can It Fit app, so it only works on an Android phone that has the app installed. Install the APK, then come back to this page and tap it again.

About the sizes

Every size is measured at the widest point. The sofa, lounge chair and TV console come with full 3D models. The bed, desk and bookshelf are built from their sizes, so they look plainer in the app but measure exactly the same.

Nook & Co. is a demo store made for Can It Fit, a school project. Nothing here is for sale. Models and photos of the sofa, lounge chair and TV console: Poly Haven (CC0).
